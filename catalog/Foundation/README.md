# Foundation Models & Reusable Backbones

[Home](../../README.md) · [All collections](../README.md) 

This collection brings together 64 papers published between 2021 and 2026 on foundation models and reusable backbones for medical visual question answering (Med-VQA) and related multimodal learning tasks. The papers are grouped into three categories: Reusable Visual & Vision-Language Encoders, Generalist Biomedical Vision-Language Models, and Specialty & Grounded Vision-Language Models. The collection covers medical image-text pre-training, contrastive learning, masked modeling, knowledge distillation, vision-language alignment, multimodal instruction tuning, and parameter-efficient adaptation. It includes models such as MedCLIP, PubMedCLIP, M3AE, LLaVA-Med, and BiomedGPT, alongside models developed for specific applications in chest X-ray imaging, digital pathology, endoscopic surgery, and ophthalmology. Several papers also address visual grounding, pixel-level understanding, medical report generation, and visual question answering. References are listed chronologically within each category, with publication details, DOIs, and links to available papers, code, and datasets. This collection provides a reference for exploring medical vision-language foundation models and identifying reusable backbones for Med-VQA research.
