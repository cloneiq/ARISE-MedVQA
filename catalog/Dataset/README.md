# Datasets & Data Resources

[Home](../../README.md) · [All collections](../README.md) 

This collection brings together 26 papers on datasets and data resources for medical visual question answering (Med-VQA) and related multimodal learning tasks, spanning 2018 to 2026. The papers are organized into six categories: General Medical VQA Datasets, Image–Text Pretraining & Instruction Data, Radiology & Volumetric Imaging, Pathology & Whole-Slide Imaging, Endoscopy, Surgery & Medical Video, and Other Specialties, Education & Dialogue. The collection includes datasets such as VQA-RAD, SLAKE, PMC-VQA, VinDr-CXR-VQA, GEMeX, and Kvasir-VQA, along with related resources for image question answering, image-text learning, visual grounding, multimodal reasoning, medical dialogue, and instruction tuning. The datasets cover X-ray, CT, MRI, digital pathology, endoscopic imaging, and medical videos, as well as specialty applications in dermatology, mammography, and wound care. Papers are listed chronologically within each category, with bibliographic details, DOIs, and links to available datasets, code, and related resources. This collection provides a reference for finding and comparing existing data resources used in Med-VQA research.
