# Methods & Algorithms

[Home](../../README.md) · [All collections](../README.md)

This collection contains 203 research papers on medical visual question answering (Med-VQA) and related methods, covering work from 2019 to 2026. The papers are organized into seven categories: Architectures & Multimodal Fusion, Representation Learning & Cross-modal Alignment, Knowledge-enhanced & Retrieval-augmented Methods, Prompt Learning & Efficient Adaptation, Reasoning & Answer Generation, Reliability, Generalization & Debiasing, and Agentic & Tool-augmented Systems. The collection covers a range of approaches, including attention mechanisms, Transformers, contrastive learning, knowledge graphs, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), parameter-efficient fine-tuning, chain-of-thought reasoning, causal interventions, and multi-agent collaboration. The studies address applications such as medical image question answering, pathology analysis, surgical scene understanding, and diagnostic assistance. Papers are listed chronologically within each category, with titles, authors, publication details, DOIs, and links to available papers, code, datasets, and other resources. This collection provides an organized reference for exploring existing Med-VQA methods, finding relevant studies, and tracing the development of different technical approaches.
