# Benchmarks & Evaluation

[Home](../../README.md) · [All collections](../README.md)

This collection brings together 36 papers on benchmarks and evaluation methods for medical visual question answering (Med-VQA) and related multimodal AI systems, covering work from 2018 to 2026. The papers are organized into three categories: Benchmark Resources & Shared Tasks, Capability & Comparative Studies, and Reliability & Evaluation Protocols. The collection includes benchmarks such as ImageCLEF VQA-Med, OmniMedVQA, GMAI-MMBench, GEMeX, CARES, and MedFrameQA. These resources cover medical visual question answering, visual grounding, multi-image understanding, 3D spatial reasoning, clinical reasoning, and the broader capabilities of medical multimodal models. Other papers compare model performance and adaptation methods or examine evaluation issues such as robustness, trustworthiness, confidence calibration, consistency, and evaluation protocols. The studies span radiology, pathology, ophthalmology, endoscopy, and surgical applications. References are listed chronologically within each category, with publication details, DOIs, and links to available benchmarks, datasets, code, and related resources. This collection provides a reference for selecting Med-VQA benchmarks, comparing models, and designing evaluation experiments.
