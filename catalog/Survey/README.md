# Surveys & Reviews

[Home](../../README.md) · [All collections](../README.md)

This collection brings together 23 survey and review papers published between 2023 and 2026, covering medical visual question answering (Med-VQA) and related areas of multimodal AI in healthcare. The papers address topics such as medical vision-language models, foundation models for medical imaging, multimodal large language models, and the datasets, architectures, and methods used in Med-VQA research. They also explore specific approaches, including generative models, CLIP-based learning, knowledge fusion, knowledge distillation, and teacher–student learning. Several reviews focus on broader issues such as medical hallucinations, model reliability, AI agents, and medical report generation, while others examine applications in ophthalmology, colonoscopy, and robotic surgery. The references are organized chronologically, with bibliographic details, DOIs, and links to available papers, code, and other resources. This collection serves as a reference for locating relevant surveys and understanding the existing literature on Med-VQA and medical multimodal AI.
