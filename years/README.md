# Browse by Year

[Home](../README.md) · [All collections](../catalog/README.md) 

This is the year index for ARISE-MedVQA, a collection of papers on medical visual question answering. Each year has one table, with the newest listed paper dates first. The year a paper is cited under can differ from the date it appeared online and from the year in its paper ID. This happens, for example, when conference proceedings carry a later citation year. Follow a year's citation link to see the full entries. Click a paper ID to read its note, or use the last column to open the DOI or paper page. The category catalog groups the same literature by contribution, so you can browse datasets, benchmarks, methods, foundation models, or surveys separately. The early papers explain how researchers collected image questions and set up shared tests. Later work extends the task to specialties such as pathology and surgery, trains models on medical images and text, and asks systems to locate findings or use evidence from more than one image. If you are comparing results, check the detailed citation and note for the image source, question type, and scoring rules. A score for choosing an answer from a list tells you little about whether a free-text explanation is correct or whether the model located the right finding.
